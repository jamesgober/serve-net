# serve-net v0.4.0 - Extended

**Deployment.** v0.4.0 runs serve-net where real services run: behind any front end, on a Unix socket, under systemd, and through a restart or a configuration change without dropping a connection. It also fixes two defects in 0.3.0.

## What's new

- **PROXY protocol.** `ProxySource::proxy_protocol`: v1 and v2 from trusted peers only, with `LOCAL` health checks and the SSL and authority fields. A peer outside the list is closed before anything is read.
- **Unix sockets.** `ListenerSpec::unix` with file permissions and `UnixPeerTrust`, checked against the peer's credentials before anything is read. A proxy on the socket can name the client.
- **systemd and hand-off.** `ListenerSpec::inherited` with `fallback_bind`, `ListenerSpec::handed_off`, and `SystemdNotify` in safe `std` code.
- **Live reload.** `Running::reload` is all or nothing: validation first, new listeners bound before anything is published, and open connections pick up new limits and ingress at their next request.
- **Restarts.** Two processes share the listening socket; the test runs a restart under load with no request refused or reset (Linux and macOS; Windows follows net-io 0.5).
- **The TLS seam.** `Ingress::DirectTls` and `ServerBuilder::tls` take any `net_io::TlsAcceptor`.
- **`docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`**, and a CI job that runs real nginx, Apache, and HAProxy in front of serve-net.

## Fixed

- **Timeouts closed silently.** On real sockets a head, body, or reply timeout closed the connection without the 408 or 503 the design calls for. The refusal is now flushed first.
- **Limits that never reached net-io.** `min_rate`, `max_pending_output`, and `linger` kept net-io's defaults, and net-io's 30 s read timeout could cut off a longer `request_timeout`. All are mapped now, with `transport_limits` to override.

## Also from the 0.3 API

An audit of `docs/API.md` against the source found items 0.3.0 promised and missed: `ServerBuilder::transport_limits` and `clock`, `ListenerSpec::listen_options` and `transport_limits`, `HttpRequest::detach_head` with `DetachedHead`, and the observer's drain, capacity, and public-bind events. They ship here, and the audit is now a release step.

## Moved to 0.5.0

FastCGI, WebSocket upgrades, `Http1OrH2ByAlpn`, and an end-to-end TLS test wait for fcgi-net, ws-net, and tls-net, which are not built yet. A run under real systemd moves with them. The hooks they plug into are in this release.

## Known limits

`InFlightCap` budgets keep their start-up values across a reload. PROXY trust and TLS are fixed when a listener is bound, so a reload refuses to change them in place (`RuleId::ReloadListener`). A failed `bind` closes inherited and handed-off sockets rather than returning them. Each is tracked in the pre-1.0 polish register in `dev/ROADMAP.md`.

## Upgrading from 0.3.0

A custom family's `head` takes `&HeadContext<'a>` (the head's own lifetime). `ListenerSource` and `Ingress` have new variants; both are non-exhaustive. serve-net now depends on net-io 0.4.

## What's next

`0.5.0`: net-io's reactor driver, streaming bodies, `Offload`, and FastCGI and WebSocket as their crates arrive.
